To my dearest pretty laaaaaablaaab,

Happy birthday, my love! Today is your day, and you are sooo pretty, laaaaaablaaab—just like always. I'm so happy nga nagkuyog ta and that we got to celebrate your birthday together. Sunod, I'll make sure nga mas mapa-happy pa nato imong celebration.

Thank you, love, for everything. Layo na ta'g naabot, pero layo pa ta'g journey—and through every part of it, I'll be with you always. I will love you no matter what happens. I want to keep putting a smile on your face and loving you for the rest of my life. I know words aren't enough, but I promise nga paningkamotan nako nga tinuod ang tanan nakong gisulti.

You light up my world, laaaaaablaaab, and you are so important to me. I want to see you smiling and being your true self. Naa gyud tay ups and downs, but remember: sa tanang seasons nga atong nalabyan ug malabyan pa, naa ra gihapon ko sa imong kilid. I may not always know what you're struggling with, but naa ra ko pirmi—ready maminaw, mo-comfort nimo, ug moatiman nimo.

I treat you as my queen and my princess, and no one could ever replace you. I'm so sure of us, love. I know we're still young, and marrying each other takes time and patience. I'll wait patiently, keep improving myself, and work toward becoming a better boyfriend—and someday, husband. I have my imperfections, laaaaaablaaab, but I'm willing to grow into a better version of myself and build a life with you, especially with God at the center of it.

Kapila na nako ni gisulti, but I really mean it: tinood ang tanan nakong gipang-ingon. Bawi ra ko, ha, love. Thank you for staying and for choosing me despite our arguments and the problems we've been through. Even when we're both tired—physically or mentally—naa ra gihapon ko sa imong side. We can rest together, love.

I may not have a physical gift for you, but I can give you my loyalty and my love, for as long as I live. I see my future in your eyes, and I want to stand by your side while we reach our plans and dreams together.

I love everything about you: how you treat and understand me; the way you smile, think, laugh, and speak; your eyes, your lips, and your beautiful personality. Ikaw ang usa sa mga rason nga nausab ko ug naningkamot ko mahimong mas maayo pa. I love how you always look for a way to fix things when we have problems—like when you helped me look for a way to sort out my tuition. Thank you for caring that much.

I love our silly moments and every moment we spend together. I love how you prioritize and care for your family and your education. I love how you love my family, too. Kasabot ko nga usahay maulaw ka, and that's okay, laaaaaablaaab—kasabot ra ko nimo, ug gihigugma gihapon tika. I love the little ways you comfort me, like patting my head or my back when I have a problem. I appreciate you so much, forever and ever, my beautiful birthday girl.

I want to see you win, laaaaaablaaab, and I'll always be your number one supporter. We'll worship God together and be thankful for His blessings and guidance. I love you, I miss you, and I'll stay by your side—always.

Happy birthday once again, my love! Amping ka always, ha. I know you might be asleep right now, but I wanted you to wake up knowing just how much I love you.

I looovee you soo much, my beautiful birthday girl ♥

Forever yours,
your love
